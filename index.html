Biography

Chicago-born, Carnegie Mellon-bound

I'm an Electrical & Computer Engineering student-athlete at Carnegie Mellon, relentlessly driven to learn, improve, and solve problems. With a growth mindset and an entrepreneur's agency and bias for action, I thrive in fast-moving environments where I can experiment, iterate quickly, and create measurable value.

As the founder of Achieve More Academics, I built a STEM tutoring business with 5+ tutors serving middle school and high school students. I handle all aspects from client management and marketing to progress reporting and payment processing, while delivering personalized tutoring sessions that maintain high academic outcomes.

Previously, I founded A+ Washing, a residential power-washing service, where I led marketing strategy, managed client communications, and oversaw workflow optimization. I'm fluent in using AI tools to optimize academic productivity and advance business ventures, combining technical literacy with entrepreneurial execution.
